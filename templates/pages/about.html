About VIDYA

Where Knowledge Becomes Power.

VIDYA is a modern online learning platform with an emerald-and-gold identity — courses, video lectures, assignments, practice tests and verified certificates, all running on a Django + MySQL-ready backend.

Our Mission

Make high-quality, mentored learning affordable and accessible — every learner can enroll, track progress and earn proof of skill.

Our Vision

A world where anyone with curiosity can build a career — guided by expert instructors, structured curriculum and honest feedback.

Our Values

  • Learner-first design
  • Real projects & feedback
  • Transparent progress & certificates
  • Secure, maintainable engineering

Why choose VIDYA?

Structured paths

Modules → lessons → assignments → certificate eligibility.

Server-tracked progress

70% lessons / 30% assignments, auto-recalculated.

Verified proof

PDF certificates with public verification codes.

Practice safely

Timed demo quizzes scored instantly in-browser.
